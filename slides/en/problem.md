## your agents write code faster than your team can verify it.

<div class="mt-[40px] grid grid-cols-4 gap-[40px]">
  <Step index="01" title="backlog">Coding agents open more pull requests than any team can check by hand.</Step>
  <Step index="02" title="maintenance">Every redesign breaks the test scripts, and the suite needs rework.</Step>
  <Step index="03" title="flakiness">Unstable runs fail at random, so teams stop trusting red builds.</Step>
  <Step index="04" title="regressions">Cases nobody scripted reach production, and customers find them first.</Step>
</div>
